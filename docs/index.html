About Runbook Radar

Explore how search actually works. Compare BM25 and TF-IDF, inspect term contributions, and evaluate retrieval against labeled questions.

An independent Python application by Muzammil Qazi, based in Pune, India.

Implementation, evidence, and scope

BM25 and TF-IDF cosine retrieval; adjustable word chunks and overlap; term-level contributions; source passages; labeled mean reciprocal rank and Hit@K evaluation.

Lexical retrieval, not a generative LLM assistant. Similarity scores are not confidence probabilities and synthetic evaluations are not production benchmarks.

The five public applications are independent portfolio projects developed with Codex assistance. They use synthetic demonstrations and do not establish employer deployment, client outcomes, certifications, or production adoption.
